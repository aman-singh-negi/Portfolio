# Aman Negi Portfolio — Premium Product Experience

## Goal

Do not build a traditional developer portfolio.

Build a luxurious digital product experience inspired by Apple, Linear, Vercel, Stripe, Arc Browser, Framer and modern award-winning websites.

The visitor should feel:

"This person has exceptional attention to detail."

The portfolio itself should demonstrate UI, UX, frontend engineering, motion design, and taste.

---

# Design Philosophy

Keywords:

* Minimal
* Premium
* Sophisticated
* Elegant
* Editorial
* Interactive
* Spacious
* Timeless

Avoid:

* Flashy gradients everywhere
* Excessive particles
* Too many colors
* Generic developer portfolio layouts
* Large icon dumps
* Heavy glassmorphism

Everything should feel intentional.
